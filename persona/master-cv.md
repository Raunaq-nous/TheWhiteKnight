## Voice and tone (how I write)

These are observed patterns from my actual writing across cover letters and outreach. CareerOS should match this voice when generating any external content.

**Sentence rhythm:**
- Short declarative openers, longer connective middles
- "That is a harder problem than it sounds" / "The honest framing is..." / "What makes this interesting specifically..."
- Direct, not corporate; specific, not vague
- Avoids "I am excited to," "I am passionate about," "I would love the opportunity"

**Argument structure:**
- Lead with the substantive claim, then the evidence
- Acknowledge the counter-position before refuting it
- End paragraphs with a punchline that lands

**What I never write:**
- Em dashes (—)
- "Synergy," "leverage" (as a verb), "cutting-edge," "innovative solutions"
- "Excited to apply," "passionate about your mission," "great cultural fit"
- "Self-starter," "proven track record"
- "Consumer-facing"
- Two distinct projects combined in one bullet

**What I do write:**
- "First principles thinking" — woven naturally into profile summaries
- Specific company and project names, not categories
- The actual numerical outcome where I have it
- The honest tradeoff or constraint, not just the upside
