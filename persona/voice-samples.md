## Patterns to extract and reuse

**Opening patterns:**
- "[X] years of [domain] work across [companies] have been spent doing exactly what this role asks for: [specific behavior]"
- "What makes this role interesting specifically is [the hard part]"
- "The AI builds are live and working. [Concrete enumeration]."

**Closing patterns:**
- "That combination is exactly where my experience sits."
- "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss [specific thing about the role] and how my background fits what the team is building."
- "[The role's actual problem] is the kind of problem I find genuinely interesting to work on."

**Connector phrases (use sparingly):**
- "On the [topic] side:"
- "The honest framing is..."
- "That is a harder problem than it sounds."
- "What I bring alongside [the obvious] is [the differentiator]."

**Words to substitute:**
| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| "Excited to apply" | "Seven years of [X] have been spent..." |
| "Passionate about" | "Find this kind of problem genuinely interesting" |
| "Leverage my skills" | "Apply [specific skill] to [specific problem]" |
| "Synergy" | (just don't) |
| "Self-starter" | (let the work demonstrate it) |
| "Proven track record" | (let the bullets demonstrate it) |
| "Cutting-edge" | (just don't) |
| "Innovative solutions" | (just don't) |
| Em dash (—) | comma, semicolon, restructure |

**Tone calibration by audience:**
- **MBB / strategy roles:** more formal, structured, less first-person
- **AI / product roles:** more direct, confident about builds, lead with concrete proof
- **Chief of staff / founder office roles:** warmer, more about partnership and trust
- **Crypto / Web3 / fintech:** acknowledge the domain gap honestly, lead with transferable mechanics
- **Aerospace / engineering:** include the IEEE publications, more technical depth signaled
