## Project context (for interview talking points)

- **Parikshit Student Satellite Team** — student-led, ISRO-guided, Manipal Institute of Technology, 2013–2016
- **Vehicle:** polar low-Earth orbit nano-satellite
- **Velocity at deployment:** 28,800 km/hr
- **My role:** ADCS Subsystem Head (Attitude Determination and Control System)
- **What ADCS does:** keeps the satellite oriented correctly in space — pointing the right side at Earth, the right side at the Sun, etc. Without ADCS, the satellite tumbles uncontrolled.
- **Specific contribution:** designed the control mechanism to counter the tether ejection event (when the satellite separates from its tether, it experiences a sudden force that destabilizes orientation; my work designed the corrective control loop)

## Note on em dashes

Some IEEE citations include em dashes between title and journal. CareerOS replaces these with periods to comply with the no-em-dash rule:

❌ "Title — IEEE, 2016"
✅ "Title. IEEE, 2016"
